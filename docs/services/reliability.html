High-Availability Enterprise Voicebot

Ensuring 24/7 uptime and flawless input recognition for critical customer service channels.

The Challenge

A telecommunications provider was experiencing severe reliability issues with their automated customer service routing. The system suffered from frequent API timeouts and critical failures in parsing complex alphanumeric inputs (like account numbers and device IDs), resulting in high rates of dropped calls and user frustration.

The DataNatus Solution

We re-architected the voicebot integration utilizing Google Dialogflow seamlessly connected with the NICE CXone platform. To guarantee reliability, we implemented comprehensive monitoring and customized error-handling logic. We specifically engineered custom fallback intents and specialized context parsing to resolve the alphanumeric input recognition errors, ensuring the system remained robust even during unexpected user inputs.

-85% Reduction in Dropped Interactions
